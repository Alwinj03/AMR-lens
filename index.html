How good is the estimator?

Tested by hiding whole country × bug–drug cells (all years) and predicting them from the rest: 5-fold grouped cross-validation. Error is in percentage points of resistance.

The additive model is country average + bug–drug average. The forest can learn interactions; it only needs to beat the additive model to justify itself.

What drives the estimate

Permutation importance: how much error rises when one input is shuffled.

Read with care

  • 2019 includes 71 countries; other years only the 46 that reported every year. Compare years within a country, not global totals.
  • 2020 coincides with COVID-19 disruption to hospital testing.
  • Surveillance samples skew toward sicker, hospitalised patients.
  • Not for choosing treatment for any patient.
